## Description (App Store ≤4000 / Play ≤4000)
```
Analog Intelligence turns photos of your film negatives into finished
positives — right on your phone. No lab, no cloud, no waiting.

Hold a developed negative over any even light source, scan it, and the app
inverts it, removes the orange film-base mask, and applies film-aware color and
tone correction in seconds.

EVERYTHING ON-DEVICE
• Your images are processed locally and never leave your phone.
• No account, no upload, no servers.

BUILT FOR FILM
• C-41 color, black & white, and slide (E-6) support
• Automatic orange-mask removal and negative inversion
• Film-stock looks: Frontier, Noritsu, Portra, Gold, Ektar and more
• One-tap white balance sampled from the film base
• Manual controls: exposure, contrast, warmth, highlights, shadows, vibrance

SEE WHAT YOU'RE DOING
• Live histogram with one-tap "fix it" suggestions
• Before/after slider to compare the raw negative and your positive
• Focus peaking and a composition frame guide while scanning

ORGANIZE YOUR ROLLS
• Group scans into rolls, add per-frame notes, export a roll at once
• Apply one frame's look to the whole roll

FREE & PRO
• Free: scan, adjust, and export with a small watermark
• One-time Pro: full-resolution export, no watermark, no ads, and Insights

Shoot film. Skip the lab. Scan smarter.
```
