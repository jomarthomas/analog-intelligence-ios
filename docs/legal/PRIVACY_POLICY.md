**Analog Intelligence** ("the app", "we") is a film-negative scanner published by
[YOUR NAME / COMPANY] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does and
does not do with your data.

## The short version
Your photos and scans are **processed entirely on your device and never leave
it.** We do not run servers, we do not upload your images, and we cannot see your
scans. The only data that leaves your device is what the third-party
**advertising** and **purchase** services below need to function in the free
tier.

## What the app accesses on your device
- **Camera** — to photograph film negatives. Frames are processed locally.
- **Photo Library** — only if you import a negative or save a finished positive.
- **Local storage** — scans, rolls, and settings are stored on-device (SQLite +
  the file system + on-device preferences). Nothing is synced to us.

We do **not** collect your name, email, contacts, location, or your images.

## Third-party services (the only data that leaves your device)
- **Google AdMob** (free tier ads). AdMob may process an advertising identifier
  and device/ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. Before any ad loads,
  the app presents Google's **UMP consent** form where required (e.g. EEA/UK),
  and on iOS requests **App Tracking Transparency** permission; if you decline,
  ads are non-personalized. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **RevenueCat** (purchases). When you buy Pro, RevenueCat and Apple/Google
  process the transaction and an anonymous app-user id to manage entitlements.
  RevenueCat's policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
- **[Optional] Sentry** (crash diagnostics, only if enabled). Receives crash
  stack traces and device/OS metadata to fix bugs — never your images.

We have no access to your payment details; those are handled by Apple/Google.

## Data retention & deletion
Because scans live only on your device, **you control them** — delete a scan,
roll, or the app to remove the data. Ad/purchase data is retained by those
providers per their policies above.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
data from them.

## Your choices
- Revisit ad consent any time in **Settings** (UMP "Privacy options").
- Revoke camera/photos permission in your OS settings.
- Use Pro to remove ads entirely.

## Changes
We may update this policy; material changes will be noted with a new "Last
updated" date.
